# Methods and claim scope

HeptadPair separates single-chain heptad organization from pair-specific compatibility. Each chain is assigned on the canonical seven-position heptad cycle; the pair representation then conditions typed-four a/d/e/g interaction summaries on relative orientation and cyclic register shift.

The current release supports three bounded claims:

1. **Frozen internal benchmark:** the representation improves over the protocol-matched iCipa implementation on CCNG1 both-chains-unseen splits.
2. **Target-domain prediction and retrieval:** HeptadPair retains useful zero-shot CCmax signal and supports strong locked CCmax-supervised prediction and orthogonal partner recovery.
3. **Cross-assay transfer:** CC0 and MP3-seq show meaningful but task-dependent transfer.

Important boundaries:

- the Z7 representation is a sequence/register representation, not exact 3D geometry;
- the framework is optimized for dimeric coiled coils rather than higher-order oligomerization;
- homodimer-rich candidate sets remain difficult;
- the optimal learner/feature subset is endpoint-dependent;
- AlphaFold2-Multimer results in Phase82 are retrospective modeled illustrations only.
